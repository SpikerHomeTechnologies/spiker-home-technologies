Meet the Owner

Travis Spiker

Founder • Owner • Engineer • Privacy Advocate

A lifelong Omaha Nebraska resident with a career spanning physical security, information technology, cybersecurity, and risk management—now bringing that experience together to help homeowners build smarter, safer, and more private homes.

Technology expertise grounded in real world security

Travis began his career in loss prevention and as a licensed private investigator, where he developed an early understanding of risk, situational awareness, and the practical measures that protect people and property. He later moved into uniformed security, helping safeguard buildings and employees through CCTV, alarm monitoring, and hands-on security operations.

His path then expanded into information technology. After working on an IT help desk and as a Network Operations Center analyst, Travis advanced through technical services—from technician to senior technician and ultimately manager across multiple call-center locations.

He continued building his expertise in cybersecurity, first as an analyst and then in management roles. Today, he works in the financial industry within information security and risk management, where protecting sensitive data, evaluating risk, and building dependable safeguards are central to his work.

Why Spiker Home Technologies?

Travis founded the company around a straightforward belief: homeowners should benefit from modern security and automation without surrendering control of their systems, recordings, or personal data to a large provider.
